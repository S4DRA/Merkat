# Merkat product constraints

People → Conversation → Plan → Meet → More Conversation.

Chat ↔ Plan ↔ Calendar stays connected. A plan retains its conversation reference; calendar is an expression of personal events and accepted/shared plans, not a disconnected calendar product. These are future requirements, not implemented features.

Design: social/playful brutalism; orange #F55105, white, off-white, black and restrained neutrals. Bold geometric typography, controlled curves, intentional negative space and behavior-driven motion. Avoid generic dashboards, glassmorphism, excessive gradients or rounded cards. Use the supplied logo unchanged.

Phase 1 uses local system sans-serif fonts to avoid a font download dependency; final Inter/Geist asset selection remains a refinement. Tokens cover color, spacing, typography, border weights, minimal radii, pressed depth and reduced-motion timing. White small text on primary orange is not used for controls; selected navigation uses white on black. Large brand display text uses the supplied orange direction.

Both classic and future central navigation share destinations. Search stays outside Central Nav. The preview labels every destination as a structural shell. No mock production data.

Source references supplied by the user: product explanation and 36-stage plan; seven Merkat brand/catalog images; approved Phase 0 review; Phase 1 instructions and architectural choices. The user's Merkat-specific direction supersedes unrelated VAL/Doshab visual defaults.
